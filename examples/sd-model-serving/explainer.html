SYSTEM DESIGN · DEPLOY AND HOST MODELS
How do you deploy and host them?
Assume a number. Say it aloud. Move on.
“One team floods the platform. What happens?”
a quota and a queue per team·over quota, only that team is rejected
“How is an LLM different?”
tokens stream back·vLLM, continuous batching·capacity = tokens per second
“Self-host the LLM, or call an API?”
both: a small Qwen model for one narrow text decision, next to a frontier model API
my rule: below about a third of the volume, do not self-host
“The registry goes down. Does serving stop?”
no: replicas already hold their weights·only new deploys wait
The design, in four sentences
1Immutable versions, on a shared inference server.
2Small models packed on shared GPUs. Critical ones alone.
3Scale on queue depth, with warm replicas.
4Shadow, canary, everyone, with automatic rollback.
